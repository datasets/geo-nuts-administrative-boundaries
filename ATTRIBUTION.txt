Contains boundaries of Türkiye that GISCO states were made using OpenStreetMap data (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Licence: unclear for this repo (none stated by GISCO for these features).

Changes made to the source data: the three GeoJSON files (NUTS levels 1, 2 and 3, scale 60M, EPSG:4326, region polygons) are downloaded from GISCO and republished with one change: the top-level "crs" member is removed. All features and properties are otherwise unchanged.
